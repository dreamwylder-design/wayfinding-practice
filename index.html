About the work

Therapy as orientation,
not instruction

I work with individual adults across a broad range of experience — anxiety, transition, grief, the harder questions of identity and meaning. The work is unhurried and shaped by what you bring, not by a method I apply to you.

01

Relational

Therapy happens in relationship. I don't sit behind a clipboard. The work is shaped by what unfolds between us — slowly, attentively, in real time.

02

Grounded & Contextual

Your story doesn't begin in the consulting room. We work with what's actually happening in your life — the contexts, relationships and conditions that shape how you experience things.

03

Unhurried

Real change rarely happens at the pace of a fortnightly session brief. We take the time the work requires — no predetermined commitment, no fixed protocol.

Wayfinding is not about knowing where you are going. It is about learning to read the terrain beneath your feet.

The practitioner

A human before
a therapist

I'm Richard Tronson. Before training as a psychotherapist, I spent years facilitating movement, community wellbeing, and self-inquiry. Those roots inform how I work — with steadiness, attunement, and a capacity to stay present with strong emotion and uncertainty.

My work is relational, integrative, and trauma-informed. The aim is not to fix you. It is to be alongside you while something becomes legible.

What I offer

Individual psychotherapy for adults — in person in Melbourne, or via telehealth across Australia.

  1. Individual therapy

    Open-ended psychotherapy for adults. Weekly or fortnightly sessions, ongoing or short-term as makes sense.

  2. Anxiety & stress

    Working underneath chronic worry, stress responses, and the patterns that keep them in place.

  3. Life transitions & identity

    Career, relationship, becoming-someone-different — the thresholds where a former self no longer fits.

  4. Grief & loss

    Bereavement, but also the quieter losses — of capacity, of relationship, of the life you had imagined.

  5. Trauma & difficulty

    Trauma-informed, integrative work for the experiences that don't yet have words, or that words alone haven't reached.

Getting started

How it begins

There is no application, and nothing to prepare. The first step is simply a conversation.

  1. Reach out

    Reach out however feels easiest — a message, or a free 20-minute call. A few words is enough to begin.

  2. A first conversation

    A short call to hear what's happening for you, answer any questions, and see whether working together feels right.

  3. Take it from there

    If we decide to begin, we book a first session. From there, the pace and shape of the work emerges between us.

You don't need to explain everything at once.
